**✍️ Authors:** Xinru Wan, Wei She, Wenke Chang, Guangping Huang, Haiwei Fan et al.
**🔗 URL:** https://doi.org/10.1111/cobi.70293

### 📄 Deep learning‐based super‐resolution reconstruction and improved YOLOv9 for efficient benthos detection: a case study at Lake Hamana, Japan

**🌐 Source:** Remote Sensing in Ecology and Conservation | **📅 Date:** 2026-03-14
**✍️ Authors:** Fan Zhao, Bangzhang Ma, Dianhan Xi, Jiaqi Wang, Yijia Chen et al.
**🔗 URL:** https://doi.org/10.1002/rse2.70066
**🎯 Relevance:** 1.00

This study proposes a dual-stage deep learning framework that combines Residual Dense Network (RDN)-based super-resolution reconstruction (SRR) with an optimized YOLOv9 architecture (V9-BENTHOS) to detect small benthic organisms from drone imagery. The approach demonstrates that enhancing image resolution via SRR significantly improves the detection accuracy of hermit crabs across varying flight altitudes, achieving a mean average precision of 95.5%.

**🛠️ Methods:** Residual Dense Network (RDN), YOLOv9, Super-resolution reconstruction (SRR), Object detection
**🌿 Topics:** benthos monitoring, species detection, aquatic biodiversity, drone-based monitoring
