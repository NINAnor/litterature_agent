### 📄 Cross‐continental zero‐shot anuran call classification with <scp>CLAP</scp> and denoising‐based synthetic embedding generation

**🌐 Source:** Methods in Ecology and Evolution | **📅 Date:** 2026-08-07
**✍️ Authors:** Ysobel Sims, Oliver Kelly, Andrea S. Griffin, Eric Johns, Alexandre Mendes et al.
**🔗 URL:** https://doi.org/10.1111/2041-210x.70384
**🎯 Relevance:** 0.95

This paper introduces a multi-stage zero-shot learning (ZSL) framework for anuran call classification using CLAP (Contrastive Language-Pretrained Audio) and denoising-based synthetic embedding generation, enabling cross-continental species detection without labeled data. The method addresses rare species monitoring by synthesizing acoustic embeddings to overcome dataset limitations.

**🛠️ Methods:** Zero-shot learning (ZSL), CLAP model, Denoising-based synthetic embedding generation
**🌿 Topics:** species detection, acoustic monitoring, rare species conservation

### 📄 Where will the cat cross the road? Comparing camera and GPS-based models for identifying wildlife corridors

**🌐 Source:** Biological Conservation | **📅 Date:** 2026-08-08
**✍️ Authors:** Read Barbee, L. Mark Elbroch, Kimberly Sager-Fradkin, Kristen Phillips, Dylan Bergman et al.
**🔗 URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biocon.2026.112029
**🎯 Relevance:** 0.95

The paper directly compares camera-based occupancy models and GPS-based step selection functions for predicting mountain lion dispersal corridors, demonstrating that stacked single-species occupancy models combined with Circuitscape connectivity surfaces outperform traditional step selection approaches in fragmented landscapes.

**🛠️ Methods:** stacked single-species occupancy models, Circuitscape connectivity analysis, step selection functions
**🌿 Topics:** wildlife corridors, habitat connectivity, species movement patterns

### 📄 Evolution of water pattern in Chagan Lake Basin from 2017 to 2025 using PlanetScope images and a newly developed MS-FCN8s algorithm

**🌐 Source:** Ecological Indicators | **📅 Date:** 2026-08-07
**✍️ Authors:** Lei Wang, Xuelin You, Kai Li, Yongkang Yu, Shaoshuai Tang et al.
**🔗 URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolind.2026.115317
**🎯 Relevance:** 0.95

This study introduces the MS-FCN8s algorithm, a modified fully convolutional network with skip connections, which integrates multi-band reflectance and water indices to improve small water body detection and boundary preservation in PlanetScope imagery. Results show MS-FCN8s achieves 2.8% higher overall accuracy than traditional FCN and outperforms U-Net/DeepLabV3+/TransUNet in complex aquatic environments.

**🛠️ Methods:** MS-FCN8s (modified fully convolutional network with skip connections), PlanetScope satellite imagery, multi-band reflectance integration
**🌿 Topics:** water resource management, habitat monitoring, aquatic ecosystem dynamics

### 📄 Combining citizen-science data with data from a structured monitoring programme for a Swiss butterfly index

**🌐 Source:** Ecological Indicators | **📅 Date:** 2026-08-07
**✍️ Authors:** Tobias Roth, Yannick Chittaro, Jérôme Frei, Glenn Litsios, Matthias Plattner
**🔗 URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolind.2026.115230
**🎯 Relevance:** 0.95

The paper demonstrates that adapted site-occupancy models can reliably estimate species trends from unstructured citizen-science data, achieving strong correlation (r=0.85) with structured monitoring data for Swiss butterflies. This approach enables cost-effective multi-species index (MSI) development by integrating large-scale citizen observations with targeted surveys.

**🛠️ Methods:** site-occupancy modeling, data integration
**🌿 Topics:** species trend estimation, biodiversity monitoring

### 📄 Deciphering riverine CODMn sources: driver identification and carbon cycle impacts

**🌐 Source:** Ecological Indicators | **📅 Date:** 2026-08-08
**✍️ Authors:** Yingxin Shang, Zhidan Wen, Ge Liu, Chong Fang, Xiangfei Yu et al.
**🔗 URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolind.2026.115312
**🎯 Relevance:** 0.70

This study develops XGBoost-based remote sensing models using Sentinel-2 imagery to monitor riverine COD Mn across China, achieving R² > 0.80 for major rivers. The models provide cost-effective, large-scale COD Mn estimation to address water quality degradation and DOC dynamics.

**🛠️ Methods:** XGBoost algorithms, Sentinel-2 remote sensing
**🌿 Topics:** water quality monitoring, carbon cycle analysis, riverine pollution assessment
